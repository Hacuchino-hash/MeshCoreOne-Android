# WP-310 deviations

## Native sheets and share handling

The Swift screens use `NavigationStack`, `ShareLink`, `UIPasteboard`, Core Image QR generation and a
camera scanner. Android exposes Material 3 bottom-sheet content with injected share, copy, QR rendering
and scan callbacks. This keeps platform side effects outside composition and avoids adding a camera
permission or scanner dependency in WP-310; the separately owned QR/platform adapter can bind the scan
callback without changing validation, slot selection or join behavior.

## Translation

Room translation state and actions remain excluded as required by the WP-310 acceptance boundary.
WP-406 owns the provider, license/privacy/runtime gate and translated message chrome.

## Integration boundary

Feature-owned dependency seams are implemented under the manifest-owned rooms/channels directories.
Application graph binding is intentionally not added here: the current app still leaves the broader
Chats feature adapter unbound, and `android/app/**` composition is a shared support capability that
must be integrated by the current app-container producer. The new route/content composables are fully
callable with real `RoomServerService`, persistence, notification and `ChannelService` adapters.

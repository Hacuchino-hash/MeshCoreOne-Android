## Architecture decisions

- **T-01 State holders, not ViewModels.** `androidx.lifecycle` is not on the feature compile classpath. Holders expose `StateFlow` of immutable snapshots, take an injected `CoroutineScope` and `TraceTimeSource` (clock + sleep), and are single-thread confined (the source was `@MainActor`). WP-302/303 provide the Android `ViewModel` wrapper and scope.
- **T-02 Dependencies are feature-owned interfaces.** `TracePathFeatureDependencies`, `SavedPathsDependencies`, `NodeDiscoveryFeatureDependencies` follow the `XFeatureDependencies` pattern. Providers are read live at each use. WP-303 binds them.
- **T-03 Type-placement mirrors (types living in core:services or other WPs).**
  - `TraceResponse(traceInfo, radioId?)` mirrors WP-209 `AdvertisementEvent.TraceResponse` (radioId nullable here because the source handler accepted nil). WP-303 maps `AdvertisementService.events()` onto `TracePathFeatureDependencies.traceResponses()`.
  - `TraceNodeDirectory` / `NodeDiscoveryDirectory` are subsets of `ContactPersisting.fetchContacts` and `DiscoveredNodePersisting.fetchDiscoveredNodes` (core:contracts, SnapshotList narrowed to List).
  - `TraceSender` / `NodeDiscoverySession` are subsets of `DiagnosticsSessionOps.sendTrace` and `MeshCoreSession.sendNodeDiscoverRequest` + `events()`.
  - `NodeDiscoveryContactAdder` is the `ContactService.addOrUpdateContact` subset; `classifyAddFailure` lets WP-303 map `ContactServiceError.ContactTableFull` without importing core:services.
  - `NodeNameMatchKind`/`NodeNameResolution` mirror WP-213 rendering types; `TracePathHop` mirrors WP-311 `PathHop`; `HopCodeParser`/`HopCodeClassification`/`CodeInputResult`/`RecentHopsStore` mirror WP-311 (the shared hop picker). `RecentHopsStorage` replaces `UserDefaults` (key prefix `pathEdit.recentPublicKeys.` frozen, UUID uppercase, lowercase hex).
  - `FloodTraceTimeout` mirrors only the WP-316 `.flood` profile. `TraceMapPinFilter` mirrors the Favorites/Discovered subset of WP-312 `MapFilterState` (trace host seeds Discovered on).
  - When WP-311/312/213/316 land, swap the mirrors for the shared types; behavior is pinned by the ported tests.
- **T-04 Localized copy** is injected (`TracePathStrings`, `SavedPathsStrings`, `NodeDiscoveryStrings`); the UI binds them to core:l10n. English fixtures in tests come from `en.lproj`.
- **T-05 Failures are reported, not dropped.** The source logged store/session failures; `TraceDiagnostics.failure(op, error)` receives them (WP-303 binds to the app logger). `CancellationException` is always rethrown.

## Behavior adaptations

- **A-01 Distance.** `CLLocation.distance(from:)` is replaced by `GeoDistance` (WGS-84 Vincenty). Oracle vectors (`evidence/WP-314/oracles/distance.*`) agree within 0.005 (asserted). A haversine alternative differed by up to 0.9%, so it was rejected.
- **A-02 Foundation text.** `SwiftText` reproduces `trimmingCharacters(in: .whitespaces)` (measured set: Zs + U+0009 + U+200B, not newline/BOM), `Character` splitting (`\X`), `isHexDigit` (accepts fullwidth, `UInt8(radix:16)` rejects it), `uppercased()` (`ß`->`SS`) and canonical-equivalence de-duplication. Oracles: `text`, `ws`, `codes`.
- **A-03 Collation.** `SourceCollation` reproduces `localizedStandardCompare` (numeric runs, case-insensitive first, lowercase first on tie) and `localizedCaseInsensitiveCompare` with `java.text.Collator`; vectors asserted. ICU data can differ from Foundation for exotic locales.
- **A-04 Batch cancellation.** The source awaited a non-cancellable continuation. Kotlin propagates caller cancellation, so `TraceBatchRunner` resets running/correlation state on cancel, and skips waiting if a cancel or response landed while the send was suspended (the source leaked its continuation there). Correlation fields are cleared before the batch deferred resumes (the source resumed first but the resumed task ran later on the main actor).
- **A-05 Discovery lifecycle.** A generation guard stops a superseded/stopped scan from finishing over its successor or setting its error; the timeout is scan-scoped and cancelled in `finally`.
- **A-06 Durations/counts.** Swift `Int` RTTs are `Long`; `traceHashMode` is `UByte`; shift saturates instead of trapping for mode >= 31.
- **A-07 Map.** `TraceMapPathLogic` ports path membership, pin visibility, tap routing (last hop removes, middle rejected) and the 10 m location rebuild gate. Lines, SNR badges, camera, `MapLine`/`MapPoint` are deferred to the UI/map WP.
